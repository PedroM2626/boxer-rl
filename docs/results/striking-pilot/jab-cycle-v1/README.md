# Explicit jab-cycle prototype v1: rejected as a positive target

All sixteen planned corner/seed/swap conditions are
[independently reviewed](analysis.json), including native trace hashes, finite
arrays, exact impulse-event totals, the complete prospective candidate predicate,
physical duration, consumed counters, unchanged foundation/main provenance and
empty native error output. This is a scripted diagnostic, not a learned policy.

**Result: 0/16 complete survivors, 0 feasible episodes, 0 qualifying candidates.**
First-fall durations range from 4.152 to 7.238 seconds. The job consumed 4,048
controls, below its 8,000 ceiling because every first-fall episode ended early.
The unused allowance is not automatically continued. No optimizer or BC update,
policy adoption, promotion, gate change or threshold relaxation occurred.

Seventeen legacy events came from the commanded left hand (22 total contacts
across both agents). Of those seventeen, one meets the speed reference, thirteen
the forward reference, five the pre-window travel requirement, nine the return
distance requirement and ten the contact-release requirement. No event meets
the complete prospective conjunction. All seventeen post-event windows are
complete, so the result is not explained by recovery-window censorship alone.
These measurements show some more frontal motion than the old target, not an
accepted stroke cycle or a validated training example.

Eight acting-hand contacts occur during inferred extend commands and nine during
inferred retract commands. Phase is inferred from the recorded approach-control
count and fixed clock; it does not establish force attribution or an isolated
cause. The mismatch is compatible with tracking lag/interaction and should be
investigated before treating a scripted phase as an executed punch.

## Decision and limitations

Reject v1 as a positive training target. Do not imitate it, extend unchanged PPO,
restore an earlier actor or lower the candidate thresholds to produce a pass.
One failed prototype does not prove that the frozen motor or original arm box
can never execute a punch. The sample is a known-seed feasibility test, not a
learned-policy gate or generalization estimate.

Next check the proposed guard/strike targets for geometric reachability inside
the actual motor-centered residual limits and investigate first-fall/phase
tracking before defining a bounded controlled redesign. A geometric workspace
check alone would not establish balance or physical feasibility. Full root/leg
first-fall traces were not saved by this arm-focused prototype, so the cause of
all sixteen falls is not established; do not call them numerical failures or
assume faster arm motion alone caused them. No new allocation is declared here.

The [prospective protocol](../../../striking-jab-cycle-feasibility.md) remains
unchanged. Formal diagnostic simulation is now **2,030,927**; diagnostic PPO
**2,163,968**, main boxing **1,048,576**, foundation **474,992,640**, combined main
**476,041,216** and standalone BC **8,000** are unchanged. No job is active.
